How to match a wheel rim to a tyre

TECHNICAL GUIDE

Two matching systems, not one

Every rim-to-tyre pairing is governed by two independent checks. The rim must agree with the tyre on bead seat width and diameter, and with the machine hub on mounting geometry. Most wrong orders happen because only one of the two was checked.

  • Rim to tyre — a W15 × 28 rim carries a 16.9-28 tyre; a 17.0-25 rim carries a 20.5-25 tyre. The tyre manufacturer’s fitment data is the final authority, and our catalogue pairings (“For 16.9-28”, “For 20.5-25”) follow the same convention.
  • Rim to hub — center bore, PCD, bolt hole count and diameter, and offset must match the machine. This is where drawings matter, and where most mismatches occur.

A rim that fits the tyre but not the hub is the most common reason for returns in this trade. Confirm both systems before ordering.

Reading the three designation families in our catalogue

W×D — agricultural designations (W10 to W18 series). “W15 × 28” means rim width code W15 over a 28-inch diameter. Larger width codes carry wider tyres — a W16 × 38 runs an 18.4-38 tyre, while a W12 × 28 runs a narrower section. If your old rim is unreadable, measure the inside width between the bead seats and the diameter, then match against the agricultural wheels range.

Diameter-section — OTR and loader designations (17.0-25, 25-inch series). “17.0-25” reads as a 17.0-inch nominal width over a 25-inch rim base, carrying a 20.5-25 tyre. The same family covers 14.0-25, 19.5-25 and 22.0-25 — all 25-inch OTR rims, but for different tyre sections. Note the structure type as well: multi-piece OTR assemblies use side and lock rings that are not interchangeable between designs, so always confirm structure with the supplier. See the construction wheels range.

Letter-number — industrial designations (3.00D-8, 5.00F-10). “3.00D × 8” means a 3.00-inch rim width, D profile designation and 8-inch diameter — a forklift rim for 5.00-8 tyres. The letter (D, F, S, T, V) encodes the rim profile, meaning bead seat shape and ledge dimensions. Check the profile letter when replacing a rim, not only the width and diameter. Browse forklift wheels for split and one-piece options.

The four hub dimensions to confirm

  • Center bore — the pilot diameter that locates the wheel on the hub, measured in millimetres.
  • PCD (pitch circle diameter) — the diameter of the circle through the stud holes, in millimetres.
  • Bolt pattern — hole count multiplied by hole diameter (for example 10 × 23 mm on our W15 × 28).
  • Offset — the disc-to-centerline distance; it sets tyre clearance against fenders and linkages and is confirmed against a drawing.

For measurement methods with step-by-step instructions, see How to Measure PCD, Center Bore and Offset, and prepare your enquiry with What to Include in a Wheel Enquiry.

Sending a matchable enquiry

The fastest quotation comes from an enquiry that states the equipment model, the tyre size and the four hub dimensions — or simply attaches a drawing, or a photograph of the old rim with a tape measure across the beads. JABIL RUBBER reviews every enquiry against these dimensions before quoting.
